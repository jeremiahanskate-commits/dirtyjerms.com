BEHIND THE SCENES

BUILT. BROKE. FIXED. REPEAT.

Welcome to the workshop side of Dirty Jerm’s. The Goods is what we make. The Records Ledger is what we learn. Back of House is where the dirty work gets done.

01 / THE WORKSHOP

WHERE IDEAS BECOME ACTUAL SHIT.

Around here, we don’t just dream shit up. We make it. Sketches become 3D-printed prototypes, prototypes become problems, and problems become better products. We build, test, rethink, and rebuild until something earns its place on The Goods.

02 / THE TESTING GROUNDS

REAL PLANTS. REAL-LIFE TESTS.

No imaginary results or fancy promises. We try our ideas with real plants in everyday growing conditions, paying attention to watering, upkeep, durability, and whether they actually make plant care easier. If something isn’t working, it goes right back to the drawing board.

03 / THE MAKING OF DIRTY JERM’S

SMALL BUSINESS. BIG-ASS AMBITIONS.

Dirty Jerm’s wasn’t born in a boardroom. We’re figuring out manufacturing, suppliers, packaging, and everything else that comes with building a plant-care company from the ground up. One decision, one prototype, and one lesson at a time.

04 / THE SCRAP PILE

NOT EVERY IDEA GETS A SEAT AT THE BAR.

Some ideas are winners. Others end up in the scrap pile. Failed prints, stubborn prototypes, and questionable first attempts are all part of the process. Failure isn’t the end of the road—it’s often where we figure out what the hell we’re doing.
