OKAY, HERE'S THE ACTUAL STORY

Born from plants.
Raised in the dirt.

Dirty Jerm's started the same way most good ideas do: too many plants, too many opinions, and somebody saying, ‘I could make that better.’ So here we are.

We design, prototype, print, test, break, rebuild and swear at things until they work. The goal is plant gear that actually helps plants climb, root, unfurl and grow — without sucking all the fun out of owning them.

And you're here early, which means you get the good seat. You get the ugly prototypes, the wins, the ‘well, that was stupid’ moments, the new leaves, the late-night ideas and the whole damn ride with us.

IF IT WORKS, HELL YEAH.

WHY DIRTY JERM'S?

BECAUSE GROWING

We like useful things, weird ideas, loud laughs, dirty hands, people who ask questions, and plants that refuse to behave. This place is allowed to have a personality. Actually, that's sort of the whole damn point.

We're here for the new leaf. The aerial root that finally grabbed on. The prototype that somehow worked. The beer on the bench. The dirt on the floor. And the people who get way too excited about all of it with us.
