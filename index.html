WELCOME TO THE FARM, KINDA.

Built with dirt under our nails.

Dirty Jerm's is a small maker brand born from plants, problem-solving, and the belief that useful things don't need to be boring. We're building plant accessories that help plants do what they already want to do—climb, root, grow, unfurl and generally cause trouble.

We're early. We're experimenting. Some prototypes will be ugly. That's part of it.
